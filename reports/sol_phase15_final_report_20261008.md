Native rating/context crosses improved standalone CatBoost on both tested folds without a replicated contribution to v6. Adding fold-safe expected ratings to TabPFN produced a three-fold mean portfolio gain of only 0.000006306. None qualifies for a new submission or Champion B in this cycle.

Selected-row scores use each candidate's matched v6 baseline. They cannot be ranked against the full five-fold v6 AUC. Test correlations for the new trained candidates are unavailable because none passed the gates for test inference. OOF correlation is not a substitute.

The [zero-training audit](sol_phase15_zero_training_20261008.json) found that the predeclared 35/65 and 65/35 route/auxiliary alternatives lost 0.000016256 and 0.000064129 respectively; each lost on all five folds. Thus 50/50 remains best among the three fixed sensitivity choices. This establishes no global weight optimum. The two historical classical diagnostics gained 0.000014135 and 0.000019079, both positive on all five folds but below the predeclared 0.000030 clean-replay trigger. Historical OOF defects prevent promotion from these gains.

The [full raw/route result](sol_phase15_raw_primary.json) has fold gains of +57.496, +16.716, +2.719, +21.518 and +13.407 millionths of AUC. Its mean paired gain is 22.372 millionths, paired fold SE is 9.309 millionths, and the ratio is 2.403, below the required 2.5. The 200-resample paired bootstrap interval is [8.628, 34.766] millionths, conditional on saved predictions. The pooled gain also falls below the 30-millionth performance floor frozen before replication. No gate was relaxed.

The [raw private-split simulation](sol_phase15_raw_private_sim.json) used 200 stratified, 200 fold-aware and 420 segment-stressed 20/80 partitions. Private median gains were 21.495, 22.965 and 21.489 millionths, with 4, 3 and 5 reversals respectively. All nine reported passenger-segment gains were positive. OOF Spearman correlation with v6 was 0.999636; sampled pair rescue/damage counts were 1,307/1,253 out of two million sampled positive-negative pairs. These are conditional resampling and Monte Carlo diagnostics, not knowledge of private labels or refitting uncertainty. They do not override the admission failure or establish an independently useful hedge.

The [native39 result](sol_phase15_native39_twofold.json) used the exact frozen Plain CatBoost seed, parameters, auxiliary features and inner early-stopping split. Standalone gains were +164.279 and +54.006 millionths. Actual v6 slot-replacement gains were +9.512 and -2.574 millionths; their mean was +3.469 millionths, with a bootstrap interval of [-3.445, +10.186]. The same sign change occurred when replacing the slot in the raw/route portfolio. The standalone mechanism worked on both folds, but its portfolio benefit did not replicate. This is a valid experiment, not an invalid-control negative.

The [expected-rating result](sol_phase15_auxpfn_threefold.json) preserved the checkpoint, seed 1201, route categorical handling and inference settings. FIT rating expectations came from inner out-of-sample probabilities, and applied rows used models fitted only to the outer FIT population. The upstream models used the 21 covariates and no satisfaction label or id. A 100,000-row timing-only probe preceded full folds. Full fold gains versus v6 were +10.709, +4.433 and +3.774 millionths; the mean was +6.306, below the frozen 15-millionth three-fold continuation threshold. Its selected-row bootstrap interval was [-10.571, +23.553] millionths. The new TabPFN alone trailed route on all three folds. Folds 3 and 4 were therefore not started.

The targeted refresh is recorded in [the research note](../research/sol_phase15_refresh_20261008.md). Two retrieved notebooks and the original cross pseudocode selected stopping points using outer evaluation labels; their reported OOF scores were rejected as admission evidence in `research/current_meta.md`. No competitor prediction CSV, checkpoint change, seed search, full-population CUDA fit or fourth branch was introduced.

1. **Is 50/50 near-optimal?** It is the best of the three frozen sensitivity choices; a global optimum is unproved.
2. **Do historical classical models contain complementary information?** They suggest a small complementary signal, but their diagnostic gain does not justify the frozen clean-replay trigger or constitute clean evidence.
3. **Does raw plus route help?** Yes, modestly: full OOF improves by 0.000022409 with five positive fold gains. It fails admission and has no new independent or test certificate.
4. **Do the 39 crosses help?** Standalone CatBoost improves on both tested folds. Their actual v6 contribution does not replicate.
5. **Does any candidate honestly beat v6?** Raw/route beats its full OOF score. No candidate passes all requirements for promotion.
6. **Is there a credible Champion B?** No. The second finalist remains unresolved.
7. **What was submitted?** Zero Phase 15 uploads. V6 was preserved and not resubmitted.
8. **What are the public scores?** Banked v6 remains 0.96144, numeric ref 56951906. The read-only snapshot at 21:46 UTC showed rank 246 of 1,203 teams, nine submissions available and a leading public score of 0.96200. There are no new scores in this cycle.
9. **Which two finalists should be reserved?** Recommend v6 for slot A and keep slot B open. These are repository recommendations; Kaggle's final-selection state was not changed. Legacy v5 remains an uncertified fallback, not a clean hedge.
10. **What are the Git and test results?** The final full suite passed 120 tests with zero failures. The frozen raw/native experiments and auxiliary TabPFN fitter were preserved at commit `a49ad505f5ad0ec06742f714b501aef1e9886618`; each run records its execution HEAD and exact source hashes. The final evaluator and strengthened exclusion regression are committed with this closure. See [the final software certificate](sol_phase15_full_tests_v5_20261008.json) and [the closure proof index](sol_phase15_final_proof_index_20261008.json). The latest pushed HEAD is supplied in the final chat checkpoint.
11. **What remains in the research queue and budget?** All three ranked mechanisms are closed for this cycle. Nine new full model folds and one timing probe used 2.664 hours; unchanged historical controls and rating caches were reused. At model closure, elapsed wall time was 3.406 hours and 4.594 hours remained before the eight-hour deadline. The user explicitly required an early stop when the evidence failed the gates. No further training is queued; a new cycle needs a distinct evidence-backed hypothesis and fresh bounded scope.
