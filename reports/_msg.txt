PHASE 13 COMPLETE: auxiliary-task features are VALIDATED and still not admissible

The mechanism is real and it is the first genuinely positive result in this campaign. It also does not
clear the gate. Both facts matter and neither is being rounded toward the other.

WHAT THE MECHANISM IS
Every model in this campaign predicts satisfaction from the columns. This asks a different question of
the same data: fit a model that predicts ONE RATING from the OTHER columns, with no satisfaction label
anywhere, then feed the expected value sum_k k*P(k) of that rating as a feature. Two independent public
sources report it and our 285-column view does not contain it. goodpjw2008 measured +13e-5 on one
XGBoost, +9e-5 on one LightGBM, and +5.9e-5 on the stack with all five folds up; sachith7 uses "aux
expected ratings" independently. We have 48 te_ + 54 ogte_* + 2 teach_*, but our teacher predicts
SATISFACTION from the original dataset, which is a different mechanism: there is no per-rating
auxiliary target here. This is not the closed teacher branch and not the closed all21-TE branch.

Extrapolating from 6 slots to the remaining extra_trees members is REFUSED. Phase 11R withdrew exactly
that kind of arithmetic, family weight times a per-slot number does not predict blend gain, and the
measured cumulative curve is non-monotonic in slot order (fold 0 runs +0.31, +0.63, +0.69, +0.93,
+0.79, +0.71 -- rising then falling), so extrapolating from it would invent precision. p13b_final.py
therefore reports the all-slot marginal only and attributes nothing to individual slots.

A CHAOS FINDING THAT GOVERNS HOW ANY extra_trees NUMBER MUST BE READ
A sensitivity probe reran the auxiliary build on fold 0 at 400 rounds / 5 inner folds instead of
250 / 3. The auxiliary models were IDENTICAL -- mean out-of-fold accuracy 0.6998 both times, matching
to three decimals for all 13 ratings -- yet the champion's standalone AUC moved 0.961320 -> 0.961047,
a 27e-5 swing. The features were stable; the model was not.

extra_trees uses randomised thresholds and per-node feature subsampling, which makes the fit CHAOTIC
under perturbations far below the level that changes any feature's meaning. So a single fold's
standalone delta for this family is NOT reproducible, and reading standalone deltas is unsafe for
extra_trees members specifically. The MARGINAL is unaffected (+0.31e-5 and +0.24e-5 at the two
settings) because pair ranking is robust to tree-level chaos in a way one model's AUC is not. This
retrospectively justifies Phase 12 having measured its marginal rather than inferring it, and it means
the fold-0 standalone figures quoted above should be read as directionally supported, not precise.
The 5/5 sign consistency and t +3.11 survive that caveat because they are computed ACROSS folds.

THREE DEFECTS FOUND, ALL MINE
1. A LABEL-FREE CHECK THAT COULD NOT FAIL. The first version flipped every satisfaction label and
   asserted the aux features were unchanged. Impossible to fail either way: build_aux receives only the
   view matrix, which contains no label, so relabelling cannot reach it. Third instance of this defect
   in the campaign, after the Phase 10A replication statistic that returned r=1.000 for all 42 keys
   and the audit's "||" detector that reported a working treatment as inert. Replaced with a
   structural guarantee that CAN fail: build_aux's signature is asserted to contain no label argument
   and its body to reference none.
2. THE AUX FEATURES WERE MEMORISED ON FIT ROWS. A model fitted on the fit rows and queried on those
   rows learns their ratings nearly perfectly, so aux_ev_j was a near-duplicate of rating_j during
   training while remaining smoothed at validation time -- a train/serve mismatch invisible in AUC.
   Fixed with INNER CROSS-FITTED predictions for fit rows and a full-fit model for val rows. The fix
   is corroborated by the measurement: every aux feature lands at correlation 0.81-0.97 with its
   rating rather than 1.0.
   The demonstration of the gap was itself wrong twice before being right: the target was first
   appended to the design matrix (1.000 in-sample AND 1.000 cross-fitted, proving nothing while
   appearing to confirm the gap), and then, with the target removed, above-chance accuracy was
   demanded on PURE NOISE where the correct answer IS the 1/6 chance rate.
3. A SILENTLY WRONG SLOT SEED. run_phase13b.py derived slot seeds with int(s.rsplit("_s", 1)[1]), which
   crashes on xt_xt_d127_bin63 and, worse, silently falls back to the loop counter for slots with no
   "_s" at all -- giving xt_xt_d255 seed 2 when its real seed is 4. Seeds are now stated explicitly,
   cross-checked against the ledger at startup, and the run aborts on disagreement. A silently wrong
   seed is the same failure that made Phase 12's fold-0 screen untrustworthy.

A RUN-SCOPED REPORT WAS OVERWRITTEN, AND THE NUMBER WAS RECOMPUTED FROM FIRST PRINCIPLES
Running p13b twice (folds 0,1,2 then folds 3,4) left reports/p13b_runs.json holding only folds 3 and
4, so the headline existed only in two log files. p13b_final.py recomputes the authoritative 5-fold
all-slot marginal from the per-fold .npy prediction vectors, which were never overwritten, and writes a
durable record. The recomputed +0.745e-5 matches the logs.

HONEST NOTE ON THE ONE PLACE I WAS MORE CONSERVATIVE THAN THE SOURCES
The public sources fit the auxiliary models once on all train+test rows. That uses no competition label
so it is fold-safe in the letter of the rule; I rejected it anyway on a stricter ground, because a
validation row's own rating_j would then have been in its own auxiliary model's training set, making
the OOF estimate optimistic in a way real test inference would not be. Cost is 13 fits per fold
instead of 13 total, about 7.5 minutes per fold. Being stricter than the sources makes a shortfall
harder to explain away by their more permissive transductive choice -- which is the main alternative
explanation for a +0.745e-5 result that falls short of the gate.
