THE HEDGE CONCLUSION IS REVERSED, AND IT REVERSES PESSIMISTICALLY
Two independent findings, both structural:

1. MY REPORTED HEDGE CORRELATIONS WERE A SIGN FUNCTION. `base` is the MEAN LOGIT of the 59 members,
   not a probability, and the module's lg() clips to [1e-6, 1-1e-6] before log-ratios. 97.87 percent
   of base values fell outside that window and saturated to +/-13.8. I was measuring sign agreement.
   True correlations against the stored v3 probabilities: H1 0.99993, H0 0.99986, family means
   between 0.99725 (tabm) and 0.99976 (lgbm_xt). The runner now asserts base still looks like a mean
   logit so the saturation cannot silently return.

2. TEST-SIDE DIVERSITY COLLAPSES BECAUSE MEMBERS SHARE TRAINING DATA. OOF versus TEST correlation
   with v3: H1 0.99993 / 0.99995, H0 0.99986 / 0.99989, realmlp 0.99900 / 0.99920, tabm 0.99725 /
   0.99798. The mechanism is structural: every TEST-time member is refit on the SAME 100 percent of
   labels, while each OOF member saw a different 80 percent subset. Models that differ on disjoint
   subsets converge when trained on identical data, so OOF overstates the diversity that reaches the
   leaderboard.

CONSEQUENCE: NO REWEIGHTING OF v3'S MEMBERS IS A PRIVATE HEDGE. Every candidate is at least 0.997
correlated with v3, because the 59-member equal-logit average has already averaged away the
member-level diversity a hedge would have to exploit. Reweighting can only rearrange what is left,
and what is left is one signal. The most independent artifact in the store, a tabm-only prediction,
still sits at 0.99798 and at OOF 0.960797 -- 71e-5 below v3, failing the eligibility bar of v3 minus
30e-5. A genuine hedge needs a structurally different model trained under a different protocol, which
we do not have. v4 therefore REMAINS the second candidate, not because it is independent but because
nothing better exists.

   profile is all-positive with t +3.97 instead of +0.07. The control now asserts its per-fold delta
   is exactly zero; a reporting bug that makes the control non-zero should stop the run.
