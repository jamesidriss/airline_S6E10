  complete parameters, selected tree/epoch counts and prediction hashes are

  models, or use an explicitly declared full-label refit. PyTabKit's supported
  n_refit=1 trains on all FIT plus its inner selection rows at the selected

- No clean result can reuse a legacy prediction merely because its name matches.
  Source and full contract must match; use new artifact/store identifiers.
- Legacy v5 splices are diagnostics only. Admission and private simulation
  require full clean OOF, and finalists require their own test predictions.
